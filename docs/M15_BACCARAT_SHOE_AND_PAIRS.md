## Authorized rules and design

`domain/config.ts` owns explicit rules. Default: eight decks, burn enabled, cut-card reserve 14 cards, Player Pair and Banker Pair profit 11:1. This is a **Casino Project default rule**, not universal casino law. Configuration is immutable during the session and included in replay/digest.

After shuffle expose the first indicator; burn A=1, 2–9=face value, 10/J/Q/K=10 **additional** cards. Total burned includes the indicator. Gameplay tens remain zero. Burned cards cannot enter a round. Authority retains inventory size, indicator, additional/total burn, cut boundary, cursor, remaining count, generation/ID and closure state; public UI exposes no future order/physical identities/seed.

Cut position is initial size minus reserve, an index boundary with no fake playable card. A round starts only before that boundary and with at least six actual cards available. A legitimately started round completes/settles even if it crosses the boundary. Authority marks CLOSING during resolution, CLOSED at settlement; NEXT/REPEAT opens a new generation, shuffles and burns before betting, preserves credits and immutable completed history, and clears previous shoe presentation. Six-card safety is independent of the cut boundary.

M15 wagers: at most one Player/Banker/Tie main target plus optional Player Pair and Banker Pair, including side-only wagers. Selecting controls is observational; placing a second main target is rejected atomically with a clear instruction to clear/change bets. Whole-credit stakes 1–1000 per component, zero cancels; total exposure includes all components. Clear refunds all open exposure. Repeat atomically restores every previous component subject to available credits. Pair checks only the first two ranks, suits/third cards irrelevant; both can win and neither depends on the main outcome. Winning 10 credits returns 120 credits (10 stake +110 profit), using integer hundredth-credit units. Authority supplies animation amounts.

Completed history contains final totals, winner/tie, independent pair flags, shoe ID and round within shoe; no roadmap coordinates.
