Expand Developer / demo tools deliberately to verify replay/audit/settings remain accessible. Any explicit mode/new-demo reset must clearly reset simulated credits.

Record a judgment and concrete example for each:

- Is it fun enough to casually play?
- Does it feel like a blackjack game?
- Would you be willing to let an interviewer try it?
- Is the dealer central and professionally presented, with formal patrons present and your hand closest to you?
- Are stakes, net results, separate wager records and the next-round actions clear?
- Is desktop/tablet usable in one view, and is mobile scrolling/guest expansion comfortable?
